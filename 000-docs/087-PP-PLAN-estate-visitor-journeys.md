## Capture contract (proposed, not deployed)

| Purpose | Minimum fields | Storage/routing | Confirmation boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project inquiry | Name, email, problem; company optional | Contact plus distinct inquiry; owner notification | Receipt only after durable acceptance; no newsletter enrollment |
| Company letter | Name, email, specific opt-in | Purpose-scoped subscription | Email confirmation before marketing eligibility |
| Learn request | Name, email, implementation context; work link optional | Separate application and review state | Application receipt is not admission or a mailing-list subscription |
| Beacon updates | Parent/guardian name, email, specific opt-in | Separate product subscription | Confirmed adult release list; no child identity or gameplay data |

Reuse verified infrastructure, not merely Beacon's form styling. Twenty is the intended CRM destination, conditional on locating and testing the actual adapter. A CRM contact is not by itself a subscription system. Verify whether the existing service owns confirmation tokens, consent events and delivery suppression before choosing another component.

Logical records: Contact; Inquiry; Application; Subscription keyed by contact plus purpose; append-only consent events; bounded attribution; delivery/outbox record. Repeated requests must not duplicate mail, erase consent history or reactivate an unsubscribed person. Preserve both initial and latest relevant source rather than silently overwriting attribution. Record consent wording/version and timestamps. Keep sensitive application review notes access-controlled.

Transactional receipts and marketing eligibility are separate. Establish an explicit unsubscribe/suppression path, expiry and retention policy, retry ownership and delivery provider. Do not put names, emails, free-text messages or confirmation tokens in analytics, page URLs or public logs. Do not import existing contacts into a new purpose without the appropriate opt-in.
