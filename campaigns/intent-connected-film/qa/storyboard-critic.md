# Independent storyboard critique

**Verdict: REVISE.** The shared work object, company positioning, and CTA are sound. The current sequence still risks looking like a tour of separate properties because the fusion becomes visible only at 22.5 seconds, after the tour has finished.

Reviewed BRIEF.md, STORYBOARD.md, brand-research.md, and the four requested motion-video-kit references. Also checked the landing source and brand-family contract for the company method and CTA. Applied the latest user steering: this should fuse the estate into something expressive in the company's visual language; role explanations and a product tour are not requested. This is a storyboard review, not approval of rendered legibility, transitions, or live product behavior.

## Ranked fixes

1. **Make fusion visible before the named-property sequence (0–20s).** Services → Products → Labs → Demos → Learn → OMA → Tons currently reads as a required linear pipeline. **Fix:** extend the opening Intent mark into persistent connected paths; keep those paths registered through the work surfaces. Demos and Learn may reuse the same evidence artifact, but OMA and Tons should grow from the shared practice rather than appear to be products generated by a learning node. The later overview should reveal the whole composition already inhabited by the camera, not introduce a new org chart.

2. **Stop the names from becoming isolated chapter introductions (10–20s).** One full-screen slogan and one property name every 2.5 seconds creates the rhythm of a website tour. **Fix:** weave OMA, Learn, Demos, and Tons of Skills into the same working surfaces and shared paths; let names survive across boundaries as other elements transform. Give each a clear moment of emphasis, but avoid role subtitles, separate logos on empty fields, or a new heading template for every property. The connected artifact should do the storytelling. Keep one or two primary reading targets at a time.

3. **Reduce recap duplication and assign reading time by complexity (20–30s).** “Services. Products. Open source.”, “One connected company.”, and “Built. Tested. Shared.” occupy 7.5 seconds and repeat work already established. Meanwhile, the overview asks viewers to read a headline, Intent, and six properties in 2.5 seconds, including animation. **Fix:** remove the standalone three-line recap and attach those categories to the earlier branches. Spend that time on a 3–3.5s company overview and 3s CTA, adjusting the other beats on the existing 0.5s grid. Introduce overview labels before the pullback; do not reveal all six as new information. End-card copy must be completely settled for at least 1.5s at phone size.

4. **Separate a conceptual workflow from demonstrated functionality (7.5–20s).** “See it work” plus an “illustrated runnable flow” can imply that the film is showing real execution. OMA's desktop and the package also risk implying that every depicted product is ready. The “Illustrated workflow” label helps but does not validate claims. **Fix:** label the graphic a “Workflow illustration,” remove “runnable” from the scene specification, and show neutral relationships instead of invented success states. Include “Built in public” in the OMA beat if the graphic represents mixed-status projects. The promised FACTS.json was absent at review time; create it with a source for each factual label and distinguish proposed connective copy before implementation.

5. **Specify distinct compositions, not only distinct words (5–20s).** Six beats rely on an expanding selection, window, sheet, or package. Repeated container enlargement can feel like slides despite continuous geometry. **Fix:** make Build a wide branch composition; Test a close-up of registered evidence regions; Demos a dominant window; Learn a large typographic loop; OMA an asymmetric desktop-tool assembly; Tons a compact-to-expanded package fan. Keep the orange actor recognizable and occupy roughly 60–85% of the usable frame with the lead subject. Use the same layouts recomposed vertically, not shrunk in portrait.

6. **Avoid turning the estate into a literal delivery sequence (2.5–20s).** The opening reasonably follows problem → build → test. The published service method is Frame → Build → Prove → Operate, while “Learn → tools → reuse” is an editorial ordering. **Fix:** retain the simple opening but use shared paths, recurrence, and simultaneous surfaces to show a practice feeding multiple outputs. Do not add method labels or another explanation scene. Do not imply every engagement produces every property or that the named sites form a mandatory pipeline.

7. **Define the exact actor handoffs and reading landings.** “Same window” and “outline passes forward” do not yet establish which contour or coordinate survives each transition. A slow push on every beat also risks uniform pacing. **Fix:** specify the surviving orange corner/edge and its destination for each handoff. Use fast departures and decelerating arrivals, with supporting motion continuing while text settles. Reserve the slow push for reading landings and the CTA.

## Keep

Keep the finished frame-zero brand composition, applied AI engineering category, real branching-arrow identity, flat graphic approach, services/products/open-source breadth, and single “Request an outcome” CTA with intentsolutions.io. The evidence-sheet → demo → learning transformation is the strongest signature moment. Preserve it while making its relationship conceptual and truthful.
