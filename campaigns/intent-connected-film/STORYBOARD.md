12 compositions; all scene boundaries 2.5 seconds apart. Every action produces a visible changed structure. Schematic scenes 2–10 (numbered from one) carry 'Illustrated workflow'. A landscape and portrait layout share the same timeline but use distinct text scale, positions and network arrangement. No production performance states are invented.

## Revision after independent storyboard critique and owner steering

The film is a fused company brand piece, not a site tour. Intent-connected rails and the orange actor remain from the opening; property names live inside shared surfaces rather than separate introductions. The two recap beats change scale and resolve the same paths into the actual mark. The original role explanations are not on-screen copy. Names are repeated only after introduction, and the network beat is reinforcement rather than first-time reading. No imagined dashboard verdicts. The CTA remains the single primary action.
