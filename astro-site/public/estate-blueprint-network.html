The emerging international network

The proposed seventh arm connects customers, commercial originators, independent specialists and firms around work with clear responsibility.

Work / artifact flowFeedbackControl / shared supportProposed boundarySelect a node to trace its connections.

What Intent has to contribute

The coordinating layer has to provide something the engagement needs. Its role can combine opportunity development, architecture, reusable engineering, contractual accountability, quality control and operations.

  • Identify the contribution before setting the arrangement.
  • Name the customer-facing accountable party for that engagement.
  • Avoid a universal claim that Intent owns every independent relationship.

International by design, explicit in execution

Independent people and firms can operate across countries, but a shared vision does not settle the legal form of their relationship. Geography, entity type and the actual work shape the appropriate arrangement.

  • Confirm jurisdiction, entity status and authorized signers.
  • Resolve IP, confidentiality, security and liability for the scope.
  • Use legal review to support the chosen commercial model.

What still needs validation

The network is a business model being developed. Repeatable customer acquisition, committed capacity, workable incentives and sustainable delivery economics must be established through actual engagements.

  • Choose a narrow initial offer and customer segment.
  • Prove one complete engagement and its operating responsibility.
  • Measure margin after delivery, coordination, support and risk costs.
  • Expand only from evidence about demand, delivery and contribution.

Read the relationships as text

Customers, originators and delivery specialists interact through an Intent coordinating layer inside one engagement boundary. Below them are three families of possible arrangements. The model is proposed, participants may hold multiple roles, and independent business remains outside the boundary.

  • Customers → Intent operating layer: scope (flow)
  • Intent operating layer → Customers: evidence (feedback)
  • Origination → Intent operating layer: context (flow)
  • Intent operating layer → Origination: alignment (feedback)
  • Intent operating layer → Execution: scope (flow)
  • Execution → Intent operating layer: delivery (feedback)
