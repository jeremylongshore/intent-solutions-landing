01 / 08Macro / the company architecture

One company. Several ways to create value.

Intent Solutions connects applied AI engineering, reusable software, research, education, products and media around one purpose: making useful technology work reliably inside real operations.

Work / artifact flowFeedbackControl / shared supportProposed boundarySelect a node to trace its connections.

A company architecture, with room to evolve

These are functions that reinforce one another. A product or team may contribute to several functions; the map is not a fixed department chart.

  • The mission stays consistent while technologies and delivery methods change.
  • Public artifacts demonstrate capability at their own scope and maturity.
  • The emerging network is a model under development.

One piece of work can serve several purposes

A troubleshooting workflow can become a client implementation, an evaluated skill, a permitted demonstration and a lesson. Each use has its own permission, quality and maintenance requirements.

  • Private customer work stays private unless publication is authorized.
  • Portfolio discovery links to products with differing maturity.
  • Public adoption, community participation and contracted delivery are separate signals.

Products make the breadth concrete

Vertical products carry the engineering into repair diagnostics, live baseball analysis and youth soccer development. Other products package the underlying engineering methods themselves.

  • DiagnosticPro: repair diagnostics and work planning.
  • Scorecard Echo: a baseball broadcast-booth companion.
  • HustleStats: youth soccer performance tracking.
  • Bob’s Big Brain and the Intent Eval Platform: governed knowledge and evaluation infrastructure.
  • Intent Longbox: collectibles intake-to-commerce, in development.

The external ecosystem has a place

Learn links public Anthropic-issued credential evidence. Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and local models remain technology choices to assess against the work; the shared engineering standard extends across vendor ecosystems.

  • Credential evidence has a defined scope; it does not establish the whole company’s delivery capacity.
  • Vendor learning and open-source contributions can strengthen capability and relationships.
  • Tons of Skills is model-neutral at its canonical source layer; Claude Code is its currently verified-native harness.
Read the relationships as text

Six company functions surround a shared Intent Solutions practice. The mission guides the practice. An emerging international engineering and commercial network would coordinate opportunities and delivery. A shared operating spine supports every function.

  • Close the deployment gap → Intent Solutions: sets direction (governance)
  • Intent Solutions → Client engineering: deliver (flow)
  • Intent Solutions → Open source & tooling: reuse (flow)
  • Intent Solutions → Education & community: teach (flow)
  • Intent Solutions → Owned products: productize (flow)
  • Intent Solutions → Research & evidence: test (flow)
  • Intent Solutions → Content & media: explain (flow)
  • Intent Solutions → Engineering network: coordinate (governance)
